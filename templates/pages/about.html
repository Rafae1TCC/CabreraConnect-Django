Who We Are

A family business with a professional edge

Cabrera Connect was founded in 2023 by two brothers who saw a gap in the local market: businesses and homeowners across Baja California needed reliable, modern security and IoT solutions — but too often found themselves choosing between quality and affordability.

We built this company around the idea that they shouldn't have to choose. One of us brings deep expertise in hardware and physical security systems — cameras, access control, structured cabling, and on-site installation. The other specialises in software integration, IoT ecosystems, and network infrastructure. Together, we cover every layer of a modern security solution, end to end.

We started with a simple belief: if we wouldn't install it in our own home or business, we won't install it in yours. That standard hasn't changed since day one.
